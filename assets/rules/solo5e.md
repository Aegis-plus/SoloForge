# Solo 5e-inspired rules

SoloForge plays a single hero, run by you, with Claude Code as the GM. Core mechanics follow the System Reference Document 5.1 (CC-BY-4.0). The solo adjustments are SoloForge's own.

## Checks
- Roll a d20 and add the ability modifier, plus the proficiency bonus if the hero is proficient. Meet or beat the DC to succeed.
- DC guide: very easy 5, easy 10, medium 15, hard 20, very hard 25.
- Advantage: roll 2d20 and keep the higher. Disadvantage: keep the lower.
- A natural 20 or natural 1 on an ability check is a dramatic twist, not an automatic result.

## Saving throws
Roll a d20 and add the ability modifier, plus proficiency if the hero is proficient in that save. The DC comes from the effect.

## Combat
- **Initiative:** d20 + DEX, highest first.
- **Turn:** move up to your speed, take one action, and possibly one bonus action. You get one reaction per round.
- **Attack:** d20 + attack bonus against the target's AC.
  - A natural 20 always hits and is a critical hit: roll the damage dice twice.
  - A natural 1 always misses.
- **Cover:** half cover gives +2 AC, three-quarters cover gives +5 AC.
- **Grid:** each square is 5 ft, and diagonals count as 5 ft.

## Damage, healing and dropping
- Temporary HP soak damage first.
- At 0 HP a creature is Down.
- **Solo rule:** at 0 HP the hero is "out of action" (captured, left for dead, or lost) rather than killed, unless Lethal mode is on. With Lethal mode, use the normal death saves.
- Healing above 0 HP removes Down.

## Rests
- **Short rest (1 hour):** spend hit dice. SoloForge's shortcut is to heal a quarter of max HP.
- **Long rest (8 hours):** full HP, all spell slots, hit dice, and Fate back to 3.

## Solo rules
- **Hero hardiness:** max HP includes an extra CON modifier × 2 (minimum 0).
- **Fate:** the hero has 3 Fate per long rest. Spend 1 Fate to reroll any d20 you rolled, or to turn a death into "out of action".
- **Minions:** most CR 1/4 and lower foes are minions and drop on any hit. Mark them elite to remove this.
- **Encounter budget (one hero):**
  - easy: 2–3 minions, or 1 foe of CR ≤ level/4
  - medium: 3–4 minions, or 1 foe of CR ≈ level/2
  - hard: one boss of CR ≈ level, plus minions
  - Avoid action economy pile-ons: let minions act together on one initiative count.
- **Companion (optional):** one GM-run ally of CR ≤ level/2. It acts on the hero's initiative and makes no attack rolls of its own unless the hero spends their bonus action to command it.
- **Tension (1–9):**
  - Raise it when things get worse and lower it when the hero gains control.
  - Oracle doubles (11, 22, … 99) at or below Tension × 11 trigger a random event.

## Oracle
- Ask a yes/no question with a likelihood: Nearly Impossible 10, Unlikely 30, Even Odds 50, Likely 70, Nearly Certain 90.
- Roll d100. A roll at or under the threshold is Yes.
- The extreme fifth of each side gives "Yes, and…" or "No, and…".
- The fifth of the No side nearest the threshold gives "No, but…".

## Conditions (summary)
- **Blinded:** fails sight checks; attacks against it have advantage, and its attacks have disadvantage.
- **Frightened:** disadvantage while it can see the source, and it can't move closer.
- **Grappled:** speed 0.
- **Poisoned:** disadvantage on attacks and ability checks.
- **Prone:** melee attacks against it have advantage, ranged attacks have disadvantage, and standing up costs half its speed.
- **Restrained:** speed 0; attacks against it have advantage; disadvantage on its attacks and DEX saves.
- **Stunned / Paralyzed:** incapacitated; it fails STR and DEX saves. For paralyzed, melee hits are critical.
- **Unconscious:** as Paralyzed, plus it drops what it holds.

## Levelling (1–10)
- XP thresholds: 300, 900, 2,700, 6,500, 14,000, 23,000, 34,000, 48,000, 64,000.
- Proficiency bonus is +2 at levels 1–4, +3 at 5–8, and +4 at 9–10.
- On a level up, add the average hit die + CON to max HP.
